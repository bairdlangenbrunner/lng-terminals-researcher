# Qatar / Ras Laffan: which LNG trains the March 2026 Iranian strikes damaged

*Research memo, 2026-07-17. Question: precisely which trains at QatarEnergy LNG (N) and (S)
were damaged and are not currently operational as a result of the Iranian strikes and related
conflict. All URLs below passed `url_verifier.py` with value tokens on 2026-07-17.*

## Bottom line

- **All confirmed strike damage is at QatarEnergy LNG (S)** — the former RasGas plant:
  **Train 4** (RasGas II era, ~4.7–5 mtpa) and **Train 6** (RasGas 3 mega-train, 7.8 mtpa),
  together ~12.8 mtpa ≈ 17% of Qatar's 77-mtpa export capacity. Repair estimate: **3–5 years**
  (cryogenic heat-exchanger lead times are the binding constraint); Technip Energies mobilized
  for the repair work. The National reports the two trains were "written off", with the South
  site's capacity dropped from 36 to 24 mtpa.
- **No trains at QatarEnergy LNG (N)** (former Qatargas) were damaged — but the whole
  14-train complex was shut down from March 2 and is still ramping back.
- As of ~July 7, 2026: **(S) has 4 of its 5 undamaged trains producing** (~19.1 of 36.3 mtpa,
  including mega-train 7); **(N) has 4 of 7 producing** (~26.6 of 40.8 mtpa; two of the three
  Qatargas 1 trains were among the first reactivated). Force majeure on some cargoes (Italy's
  Edison) extended to mid-September 2026; 21 cargoes cancelled April–September; analysts expect
  everything except the damaged 12.8 mtpa back by ~October.

The damaged trains sit **inside multi-train GEM unit rows** — no GEM unit is wholly offline:
Train 4 is one of the three trains in **S(2) T3-5**; Train 6 is one of the two in **S(3) T6-7**.

Three independent lines pin the South-site identity: Global LNG Hub names "S4 and S6"
explicitly; The National gives "Trains 4 and 6" plus the South site's 36→24 mtpa drop (the
~36-mtpa site is unambiguously ex-RasGas); and the capacity arithmetic (4.7 + 7.8 ≈ the official
12.8-mtpa loss — two ex-Qatargas mega-trains would sum to 15.6).

## Timeline

- **Mar 2** — first Iranian drone strikes on Ras Laffan Industrial City + Mesaieed
  (retaliation for US/Israeli attacks); precautionary shutdown of the entire complex.
  Satellite analysis: no major damage from this wave.
- **Mar 18–19** — heavier missile/drone attack; "extensive damage": S Train 4, S Train 6,
  and one of the two Pearl GTL trains hit.
- **Mar 24** — force majeure declared on long-term contracts (Italy, Belgium, South Korea, China).
- **Apr–May** — phased restart planning: North site "back within a month of restart",
  South site end of summer.
- **Jun 21–22** — fatal explosion (13 dead, 66 injured) during startup at the **Barzan**
  domestic-gas facility — an industrial accident, **not** conflict damage and not an LNG train;
  QatarEnergy says LNG export facilities unaffected. (Red herring for tracker purposes.)
- **Jul 1** — force majeure to Edison extended through mid-September; 21 cargoes cancelled Apr–Sep.
- **Jul 7** — satellite-thermal tracking shows 8 of the 12 operable trains running
  (4 per complex, ~45.7 of 77 mtpa).
