# Colombia

## Resources
- UPME (Unidad de Planeación Minero Energética): `upme.gov.co` — national demand / supply planning
- Bolsa Mercantil de Colombia (BMC) / SNT — gas demand prints (e.g., ~971 MMcf/d via SNT, April 2026)
- Ecopetrol / Cenit IR: `ecopetrol.com.co` — pipelines, upstream, some terminal sponsorship
- Ministerio de Minas y Energía; Colombian energy trade media (Spanish-language)

## Research Tips
- Spanish-language sources; few English releases for early-stage projects
- **Unit convention**: Colombian sources report regas capacity in **MMcf/d (= MPCD, millones de pies cúbicos por día)**, not mtpa/bcm — convert on the way in (the audit benchmarked ~1 Bcf/d ≈ 7.66 mtpa)
- Many projects are **FSU + land-based regasification** models (Buenaventura / RDP Pacifico, Caribe LNG, likely Palermo / Amazónica), not conventional offshore FSRUs — classify the project type carefully
- **Overbuild signal**: planned import capacity may exceed ~2× projected national demand by 2030 — not all proposals proceed; apply the "sufficient information to add" threshold and watch for stale start years
- Ownership: distinguish operator vs owner vs vessel operator; "S.A.S." = simplified stock company (private, not necessarily listed)
