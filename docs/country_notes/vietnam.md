## Research Tips
- 31 Vietnam terminals in GEM (2026-07-09 export) — high activity; many proposed projects
- Multiple FSRU proposals across the country; vessel availability is a constraint
- Trade press attribution sometimes confuses identical names across provinces
- **PDP8 is the discovery backbone**: reconcile the revised PDP8 LNG-power list (Decision 768/QĐ-TTg, 2025-04-15) against GEM every discovery pass. Most "nhà máy điện LNG" entries are power plants fed by an existing terminal (apply the power-plant≠terminal gate) — but the plan is where a genuinely-missed terminal surfaces (Hiep Phuoc, found 2026-07-09).
- **Hai Linh / Le Van Tam cluster**: chairman Le Van Tam is linked to multiple LNG import projects — Cai Mep (operating, Hai Linh→Nebula/AG&P buyout), Dung Quat (Van Tuong Energy JSC), and Hiep Phuoc (HPPC). Watch this name; the same-person link recurs across provinces.
- **Hiep Phuoc LNG Terminal** (HCMC, Nha Be, Soai Rap river) staged 2026-07-09 as a genuine import-terminal miss: HPPC upgrading the 40,000 DWT petroleum port into an LNG import port (own jetty + marine unloading + onshore tanks + regas) for the 2,700 MW Hiep Phuoc LNG plant. Distinct from Ba Ria-Vung Tau terminals; first HCMC terminal in GEM. Note gem.wiki tracks it only as "Hiep Phuoc power station" (GOGPT), so the wiki cross-check does NOT flag it as a terminal gap.
- Provincial mergers (July 2025, e.g. Thai Binh→Hung Yen) make province names drift; "LNG Thái Bình" and "LNG Hưng Yên" are the same project.
- **Nghi Son LNG Terminal has no selected investor** (as of Q1 2026): international bidding failed twice, a 3rd tender round opened Q1 2026 (close ~Apr 10 2026). Recurring names (Greenwell Energy Canada, JERA/Sovico, KOSPO-KOGAS-Daewoo, Gulf Energy, SK E&S, PV Power/T&T) are bidders/proposers, NOT confirmed sponsors — keep Owner/Parent blank until an award. (SK Innovation's 2026 win is the *separate* Quynh Lap project, not Nghi Son.)
- **Vung Ang supplies Quang Trach 2 AND 3** (both GOGPT power plants, piped regas, first gas ~Apr 2029 per the Apr-2026 EVN–PV GAS framework agreement) — apply the power-plant≠terminal gate to both; they belong in Vung Ang's `PowerPlantsSupplied`, not as new terminals.
