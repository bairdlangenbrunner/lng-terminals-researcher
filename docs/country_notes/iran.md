## Research Tips

- 7 GEM terminals / 9 units, all in the Bushehr/Hormozgan gulf region; **six are cancelled legacy projects** (Iran FLNG, Kangan, North Pars, Persian NIOC, Qeshm, South Pars) — sanctions killed the 2000s-era LNG build-out.
- **Iran NIOC LNG Terminal (T100000130583, T1/T2/T3) — the one live question.** GEM holds it `construction`, but the Tombak/Assaluyeh plant has been physically stalled since ~2012 (contractors left at sanctions onset; no liquefaction trains ever delivered — only partial jetty/tank civils). Newest GEM citation is Jan-2024. **As of 2026-07 there is no project-specific news to resolve construction-vs-shelved**, and the area is an active war zone (see below). Left `construction`, HIGH-severity qa, `escalation:true` — do NOT flip status without a verified basis, and do NOT guess.
- **2026 Israel/US–Iran conflict (ongoing):** strikes from 18-Mar-2026 hit the Assaluyeh gas-treatment plants and South Pars field (~12% of Iran's gas output offline), i.e. the exact feedgas region for any Bushehr LNG. Same conflict that damaged Qatar's Ras Laffan. Real-world status of Iranian gas infrastructure is volatile — treat pre-conflict sources as possibly stale.
- Sanctions make ≥2-independent-source corroboration hard; be extra strict, lean on wire services, and expect many legacy citation URLs (kanganlng.com etc.) to be 404.
