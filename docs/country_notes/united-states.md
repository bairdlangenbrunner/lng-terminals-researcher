## Research Tips
- 208 US terminals in GEM — largest country coverage
- FERC dockets are organized by docket number; project pages cross-reference
- DOE export authorizations are tracked separately from FERC siting authority
- Frequent re-evaluation of proposed projects' status under varying federal policy
- **Offshore ≠ floating**: deepwater-port export terminals (ST LNG, West Delta) are offshore but fixed → terminals-only, no vessel record; MARAD/USCG filings are the primary source
- **Separate equity owners from gas-supply counterparties and offtakers** — a recurring confusion (Alaska LNG: ConocoPhillips / ExxonMobil / Hilcorp are feedgas, not equity; Port Arthur and Woodside Louisiana: equity vs offtake vs sell-downs)
- **Capacity gotchas**: nameplate vs peak-achievable (Plaquemines uprates), and financing-package $ vs terminal capex (Texas LNG's "$5.7B" is bank debt, not capex)
- **FID is a milestone, not a status** — several US projects took Phase-2 FIDs in 2025 (Port Arthur, CP2) that should flip the relevant units proposed→construction
