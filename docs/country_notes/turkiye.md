## Research Tips

- 5 GEM terminals: Marmara Ereğlisi, Aliağa (Egegaz), **Etki (EgeGaz Aliağa neighbour)**, Dörtyol (FSRU), **Gulf of Saros (FSRU)**. (Note: Turkmenistan's Kiyanly matches a "turk" substring — exclude it from Türkiye scope.)
- **Gulf of Saros FSRU: vessel was chartered "Vasant"; BOTAŞ purchased the vessel and renamed it "Saros" (~$399M).** Capacity 5.0 → **5.7 mtpa** (GIIGNL 2026). Keep the vessel name in the displayed terminal name per the FSRU-naming convention.
- **Etki ownership = Kalyon 50% / Kolin 30% / Önal Kardeşler 20%** (corrected 2026-07-14; Önal Kardeşler was a new GEM entity). Watch the Kalyon/Kolin/Cengiz-type Turkish construction-conglomerate consortia — ownership shares shift.
- **Systemic dead-ref problem: giignl.org citations are widely rotted across ALL 5 terminals** (and likely tracker-wide) — this surfaced first in Türkiye. Worth a dedicated citation-QC / ref-refresh sweep beyond this country.
- BOTAŞ is the operator on the state-owned terminals; Egegaz owns/operates Aliağa.
