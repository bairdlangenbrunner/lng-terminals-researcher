## Research Tips
- Conflict-affected market; only one LNG terminal in GEM (Marsa El Brega), **mothballed**
- Recent activity at Brega is **LPG / gas-distribution / logistics, not LNG export** — don't misread maintenance or first-LPG-tanker news as an LNG restart
- NOC (`noc.ly`) is the primary source; expect Arabic-language releases (language consideration)
- Mark Marsa El Brega **mothballed, not retired** — no evidence of formal permanent retirement (per the manual's definition)
