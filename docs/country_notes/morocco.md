# Morocco

## Resources
- Ministry of Energy (MEM): `mem.gov.ma` — the AMI (appel à manifestation d'intérêt) dossiers and the 2025 national gas roadmap ("feuille de route gaz") are the primary sources (French / Arabic)
- ONEE (national electricity / water utility); ANP (ports); Nador West Med S.A.
- Reuters / trade media for tender and supply-deal news

## Research Tips
- French + Arabic language sources; MEM PDFs are the authoritative roadmap
- The **2025 gas roadmap supersedes the 2021 AMI** on phasing and site selection — phase focus shifted to **Nador West Med**; the Atlantic coast (Jorf Lasfar, Dakhla) is pre-feasibility / post-2030
- GEM rows look **over-specified** vs the official roadmap (fixed locations, owners, and an "active Phase 1" framing the roadmap does not commit to) — prefer flag-for-review over trusting precise GEM values
- Capacity reported in mmscfd / bcm/y; record nominal / nameplate, not peak
