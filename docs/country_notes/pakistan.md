# Pakistan

## Resources
- OGRA: `ogra.org.pk`
- SNGPL, SSGCL IR pages
- Petroleum Division / annual Pakistan Economic Survey (`finance.gov.pk`) — good primary status source for LNG licensees

## Research Tips
- 11 GEM terminals, nearly all at Port Qasim (Sindh); only **two operating** FSRUs — Engro Elengy (Elengy Terminal Pakistan) and Pakistan GasPort. Rest are proposed/shelved/cancelled merchant-import projects (Energas, Tabeer, LNG Easy, PSO).
- **Don't over-trust "inferred shelved"**: LNG Easy was stale-flagged shelved on absence-of-news, but the Economic Survey shows it's still an active pre-FID licensee — reverted shelved→proposed 2026-07-14 (a rare non-monotonic timeline transition; flag for reviewer sign-off).
- **403 bot-blocking is common** on brecorder.com, ogra.org.pk, propakistani.pk, lngeasy.com — treat as live-but-blocked (not dead); corroborate via alternate outlets / Wayback.
- Recurring vague "Energy City" concepts (Port Qasim, Gwadar) surface as monitor candidates — underspecified (no sponsor/site), keep on monitor_list, don't stage.
