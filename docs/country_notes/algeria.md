## Research Tips
- Mature export market; mostly stable operating terminals
- Recent additions (Arzew GL3Z 2014) reflect modernization, not significant capacity growth
- **GEM project totals appear to sum retired trains** — always separate operating vs retired before trusting a terminal-total capacity (both Arzew and Skikda are affected; see Update notes)
- Municipality labeling mismatch: the Arzew complex is often called the "Arzew industrial zone" in sources, while GEM uses "Bethioua, Oran" — not a real location discrepancy
