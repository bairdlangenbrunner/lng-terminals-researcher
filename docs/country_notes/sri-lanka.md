## Research Tips

- 4 GEM terminals; only **Kerawalapitiya LNG Terminal (proposed, Western Province, near Colombo/Muthurajawela)** is live. Hambantota, New Fortress Colombo, and SK Group Sri Lanka are all cancelled.
- **Kerawalapitiya sponsor history is a minefield** — New Fortress Energy had a 2021 deal that unravelled; later involvement from Sobadhanavi/CEB's West-Coast complex and Petronet (2026 interim-supply talks). Verify the current sponsor/status carefully; several past deals collapsed. Feeds the adjacent Sobadhanavi/Yugadanavi power plant — apply the power-plant≠terminal gate.
- **Heavy citation rot** — manifoldtimes, ceylontoday, thecitizen.lk, archives1.dailynews.lk, lankanewsweb are widely dead; budget Wayback + alternate-outlet repairs. Some pages are bot-blocked AND Wayback returns an interstitial → value may be corroborated but the ref unverifiable by tooling (note in qa).
- Upstream **Mannar Basin gas (Barracuda/Dorado)** occasionally floated as future LNG-export feed — far too early for a terminal candidate; keep on monitor_list.
