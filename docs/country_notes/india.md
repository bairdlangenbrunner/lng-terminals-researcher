## Research Tips
- ~28 India terminals (39 unit-rows) in GEM (2026-07 export); split into west-coast (Gujarat/Maharashtra/Karnataka/Kerala) and east-coast (Andhra/Odisha/Tamil Nadu/Puducherry/West Bengal) for sharding.

- Adani Group's Dhamra and other terminals — separate IR site. **Watch for [ref] contamination on Dhamra** (Status/StartDate refs were once pointing at unrelated Cyprus Vasiliko articles — fixed 2026-07-14).
- **Crown Kakinada LNG Terminal is the revived Krishna Godavari FSRU project** — Crown LNG appears to use the same "Krishna Godavari LNG Terminal Pvt Ltd" corporate shell as the old cancelled record. GEM keeps them as SEPARATE records (cross-referenced via AssociatedTerminals); whether to merge is an open methodology question flagged to the reviewer, not an automated edit.
- **"LNG Alliance" already exists as a GEM entity** on an India terminal — do NOT re-stage it as new (a `--country`-filtered lookup once false-negatived it into a duplicate).
- Many east-coast FSRUs (GAIL/Crown/H-Energy Kakinada, Gangavaram, Krishnapatnam, Digha/Haldia/Kukrahati) are proposed/uncertain — verify each. GAIL Paradip EoI is dead (withdrawn 2015, no revival).
