### 1.3 Capacity units — 13% of the tracker is not in mtpa, and 5 rows are wrong

Of 1,100 LNG rows carrying a numeric `Capacity`: **mtpa 951, bcm/y 87, bcf/d 26, MMcf/d 25,
gal/day 3, MWh/d 1, TJ/d 1, tpa 1, blank 5.** That is **144 rows (13%) not in mtpa**, so **any
rollup that sums the `Capacity` column without converting is wrong**. Six of those rows are in
units with no clean mtpa conversion at all — and two are live records: **Hamina LNG (Finland,
operating) 6,000 MWh/d** and **Port Kembla FSRU (Australia, construction) 304 TJ/d**; the others
are Eagle LNG, New Fortress Colombo, New Fortress Wyalusing (gal/day) and Tjelbergodden (tpa).

The genuine errors are the 5 blank-`CapacityUnits` rows — and **every one of them carries a
magnitude that is implausible for that facility read as mtpa**, so the number itself is in some
other unit and the label was lost:

| Capacity | Terminal | Status | Why it's wrong |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 100.00 | Crib Point FSRU (Australia) | cancelled | AGL sized it around 100 **PJ/year**; 100 mtpa would exceed Qatar |
| 90.00 | Sendai LNG Terminal (Japan) | **operating** | **RESOLVED** — 90 is not on the cited page at all; it is the vaporizer bank summed, ORV 30 t/h ×2 + SMV 30 t/h ×1 = **90 t/h** send-out. ≈ **0.79 mtpa** |
| 50.00 | Sakaide LNG Terminal Expansion (Japan) | proposed | Its own operating sibling row is **1.20 mtpa**; a 50 mtpa expansion of a 1.2 mtpa terminal is impossible |
| 22.00 | Cook Inlet FSRU (US) | proposed | A Southcentral Alaska import FSRU is sub-1 mtpa; likely Bcf from the trade article |
| 9.50 | Thi Vai Phase 1 (Vietnam) | **operating** | Thi Vai Phase 1 is the ~1 mtpa PV Gas terminal; sibling rows are 2.00 mtpa and 5.11 bcm/y |

Two of the five are **operating** terminals, so these are publicly wrong numbers today.
Details and the cited source per row: `capacity_units.json`.

**Sendai is now resolved (2026-08-26).** Its cited source does not contain `90` anywhere. The page
lists the Minato plant's vaporizers as LNG気化器 ORV 30 t/h ×2 + SMV 30 t/h ×1 — **90 t/h of
vaporization**, i.e. the send-out (grid-injection) rate, with the unit dropped. That is ~788,400 t/yr
→ **0.79 mtpa**, which is what a municipal city-gas utility should read, against the 90 mtpa the
blank-unit cell currently implies. (The same page's storage figures are LNG tank 80,000 kL ×1, gas
holders 100,000 m³, LPG 148 kL ×2, berth rated 18,800 kL-class — so the earlier reading that 90 was
a tank volume in kL was wrong.) Recommend staging `Capacity = 0.79`, `CapacityUnits = mtpa`.

I fixed the wrong line in `docs/reference/gem_db_schema.md` that claimed off-catalog units appear
only on non-LNG rows — `bpd` is the only one that's actually non-LNG-only (12 rows, all Oil).
